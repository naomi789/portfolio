About me

I research technical products to increase adoption, retention, and trust.

I am a human-centered mixed-methods UX researcher who thrives in ambiguity.

Stakeholders trust me because I provide clear, concise recommendations they can act on, not just a mountain of data.

My research process

Collaborative

I treat research as a team sport, building alignment with cross-functional partners from kickoff to final share-out to ensure insights are co-owned and ready for action.

Agile

In fast-moving environments, I prioritize learning through iteration to deliver timely, good-enough-to-decide insights that empower teams to move forward with confidence.

Impact-driven

I measure the success of my work by its ultimate impact on the product and its users.
